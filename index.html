KNOW THE RED FLAGS

Scams have patterns.
Here are a few.

One sign alone doesn’t prove it’s a scam. A few together are a good reason to slow down and verify.

⌛

Urgency or threats

“Act now or your account will be closed.” Scammers rush you so you don’t have time to think.

↗
♧

Unexpected links or attachments

A link in an unexpected message can lead to a fake login page or harmful download.

↗
＄

Unusual payment requests

Gift cards, cryptocurrency, wire transfers, or payment to “protect” your money are major warning signs.

↗
◌

Requests for secrets

Never share a one-time passcode, password, or recovery phrase with someone who contacts you.
